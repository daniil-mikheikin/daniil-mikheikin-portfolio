My name is Daniil Mikheikin. I’m a UX/UI designer living in Berlin, Germany. For me, design is more than just a job: it’s my passion, my profession, my hobby, and a tool for making the world a better place.
Am I just a designer? I consider myself something more - I’m a creator who expresses myself through code and design.

My journey to UX / UI design was a long one: it began back in childhood with art school and my first Python programming book for kids. As I grew up, the field of web design evolved alongside me - we went hand in hand, and when it came time to choose a career, I chose UX / UI.

Research, prototyping, creation, and programming—that’s exactly the combination I’d been longing for. Design has become not just a profession for me, but a part of my life, my way of being, and my vision of the world.
