My journey in design began in 2019, when I enrolled in an art school as a supplement to my high school curriculum. There, I acquired basic drawing skills, an understanding of composition and color theory, and an eye for detail. By the time I graduated from high school and art school, I had already decided that my career would be in design.

While preparing for university, I taught myself a lot about digital and UX/UI design, which helped me learn the material more quickly once I started studying.

I have now participated in various projects of varying levels of complexity:
from redesigning a law firm’s website to my own project to develop the Bloom browser extension, available for download on the Chrome Marketplace, which aims to improve researchers’ efficiency and productivity, as well as address the issue of fragmented tools.

I consider the 21st century to be the “golden age” of digital and UX/UI design, because today ambitious designers have gained complete freedom to bring their boldest dreams to life, create real solutions for real people, and make the world a better place.
