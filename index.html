My mission

I am driven by a mission to develop products that break down social and economic barriers to communication.

Every project I’ve created is a story of research, analysis, and creativity. During my time working in the fascinating field of UX/UI design, I’ve had the opportunity to participate in projects of various types and levels of complexity, including the creation of an online platform for effective student collaboration, a group project where my team and I developed the interface and operational model for robotic cargo delivery in an urban environment, where I served as team lead, and the development of a new brand identity and website for a law firm aimed at increasing client conversion, enhancing the firm’s online presence, and optimizing the website’s performance.

Every project and piece of work in my portfolio demonstrates my diverse skills and knowledge, not only as a designer but also as a person sitting behind a computer screen striving to make this world
a better place.
